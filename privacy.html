Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Novelita makes short fictional stories and videos from what you choose to bring: an idea you type, the names of the people in it, pictures of them, or a WhatsApp chat. This page says plainly what happens to that material. Novelita is provided by TIM GABE HOLDING SOCIEDAD LIMITADA, Spain ("we"), which is the controller of the personal data described here.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • What you bring is sent to our server, which passes it to third-party AI services to write and animate your story. Our server does not store it. The app asks for your permission before anything is sent.
  • Pictures you add are kept on your phone and sent to fal.ai with each film, to draw characters that look like the people in them.
  • Your stories, covers, films and pictures are kept in the app on your phone, not by us.
  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing is sent until you start a story or a video and have given permission in the app. Everything travels over an encrypted connection.

  • An idea. The text you type and the names you add are sent to the AI writer, which writes a short story and a script for the video.
  • A chat. The export file you pick is sent to our server. The server reads it in memory to find the two people, to choose a selection of days from across the whole conversation, and to count habits over every message (for example who writes first, at what hours, how fast you reply). The file is then discarded. It is not written to a disk, a database or a log.
  • The selection. That selection of messages, the two first names, the habit counts and any notes or corrections you add (including an idea typed with the chat) are sent to the AI writer, which returns a short description of the two of you. Episodes are written from that description and the episodes so far. Your messages are not shown back or quoted in a story, but an episode can include small details from the chat, such as a nickname or a running joke.
  • Pictures. Pictures are used only in the looks that draw real people (such as Disney 3D and Anime), not in Viral fruit. When you start, each picture is sent to an AI vision model that writes a line about the person (hair, skin tone, eyes). When a film is made, the pictures are uploaded to fal.ai's file storage and sent to its image model, which draws a character sheet that looks like each person. The sheets are used to draw the film's frames, and the video model (on fal.ai or HeyGen) animates those frames. This happens again with each film made in those looks.
  • Covers. Made from the story's premise and the written description of its characters. Your messages and pictures are not sent.
  • Films. Made from the episode's text, the character descriptions and, in the looks above, your pictures as described. The film's sound is transcribed by a speech model to make captions. AI checks also read the script and the drawn frames and sheets to catch mistakes.

Third-party AI and other processors

We use these services to do the work. Each receives only what is needed for its part and handles it under its own terms:

  • fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc.): our main AI provider. It runs the image, video and speech models that make covers, character sheets, frames, films and captions; it stores the pictures uploaded for a film; and it forwards writing and picture-reading requests to the model providers below. Privacy policy.
  • HeyGen: may make the film's clips. It receives the drawn frames and character sheets and the script for each shot, and returns the clips, which are then copied to fal.ai. Privacy policy.
  • OpenRouter, Inc.: passes writing and picture-reading requests from fal.ai to the model provider. Privacy policy.
  • Anthropic, PBC (Claude models): writes the description, the season plan, the episodes and the film scripts, and reads pictures, reached through fal.ai or directly. It receives the selection of messages, names, habit counts, notes, your idea, pictures for description, and the story so far. Privacy policy.
  • Google LLC (Gemini models): runs quick checks on scripts and on drawn frames and character sheets, and may read pictures. Privacy policy.
  • Vercel Inc.: hosts our server, so everything the app sends passes through it in transit. It also processes technical request data such as IP address, time and path to deliver and protect the service. Privacy policy.
  • Apple: distributes the app and processes purchases.

We use these providers' business (API) services. They act as our processors or subprocessors, and may keep inputs for a limited time for safety and abuse prevention as their terms describe. We do not use your material to train AI models.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your library: stories, episodes, covers and downloaded films.
  • The pictures you added, in the app's folder (visible in the Files app), until you delete that story.
  • The description of the two of you, the character lines, your idea and corrections.
  • Your settings, language and credit balance, and a record of films still being made.

Deleting a story removes it and its pictures. Deleting the app removes everything. We have no copy.

How long data is kept

  • By us: only for the seconds or minutes a request takes, in memory.
  • By our host: technical request logs, and short error messages that can include fragments of generated text such as a character description, for a limited period.
  • Pictures uploaded for a film: in fal.ai's file storage, for the period set by fal.ai's terms.
  • Clips made on HeyGen: at short-lived links on HeyGen until they are copied to fal.ai, and as set out in HeyGen's terms.
  • Covers, sheets, frames and films: at links provided by fal.ai, which your phone uses to show and download them, and which expire under fal.ai's terms.
  • By the AI providers: as set out in their terms linked above.

Why we are allowed to process it

Under the GDPR and similar laws we rely on: your consent, asked in the app before anything is sent to third-party AI, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings (the app then asks again before your next story or video); performance of a contract, to deliver the story, cover or film you asked for; and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse. Withdrawing consent stops new stories and videos from being sent. To stop the pictures in an existing story from being sent with its future films, delete that story.

Other people in your chat or pictures

A chat has two sides, so it contains another person's personal data, and a picture shows a real person. Only bring a chat you are part of, with the other person's consent, and only pictures of people who agree. Never bring a chat or pictures involving a minor. The stories are invented; they are not a record of what anyone said or did. If you believe your messages or your picture were used without your consent, contact us. We store nothing, but we will help where we can.

Sensitive information

Chats can contain private details such as health, beliefs or intimate matters. We do not ask for this and the writer is instructed to keep stories free of it, but the selection is read by the AI writer. Only bring a chat you are comfortable having processed as described here.

Where data is processed

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Purchases

Credit packs are billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. We receive only what is needed to confirm a purchase.

Permissions

The app uses the system photo and file pickers, so it only ever sees the pictures and the file you pick. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone or messages.

Tracking and sale of data

We do not track you across apps or websites, do not show ads, and do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in US state privacy laws such as the CCPA.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and handled in memory on our server, which does not store it. Pictures on your phone are kept with iOS file protection. No method is perfectly secure, so please bring only what you are comfortable sharing.

Children

Novelita is rated 13+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process children's personal data. Do not use it with conversations or pictures of minors.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es). Because we keep no accounts and store no content, we usually hold nothing to look up, but write to us and we will answer within one month.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the change affects what is sent to third parties, the app will ask for your permission again.

Contact

Questions, requests about your data, or a concern about a chat or picture that includes you: novelita.app@gmail.com
