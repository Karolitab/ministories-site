Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Ministories writes fiction about two people from what you choose to bring: a chat, a couple of pictures, or a few lines. This page says plainly what happens to that material. Ministories is provided by TIM GABE HOLDING SOCIEDAD LIMITADA ("we").

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your chat file never leaves your phone as a whole. A selection of messages is sent once to write your story and is not stored by us.
  • Your stories, covers and films live in the app on your phone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The chat export you pick. The app reads it on the device.
  • Your library: the stories, episodes, covers and films the app has made for you.
  • Your settings, language and credit balance.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We have no copy.

What is sent to write a story

When you ask for a story, the app sends our server what the writer needs:

  • From a chat: a selection of messages sampled from across the whole conversation, the two first names, and counts that describe your habits in the chat (for example who writes first, or at what hours), computed over every message on your phone. The full chat is not uploaded.
  • From pictures: the one or two pictures you pick and any notes you add.
  • From a few lines: the text you type.
  • Any corrections you add, the genre and the language you chose.

This happens once per story, to build a short description of the two of you. Later episodes are written from that description and the episodes so far, not from your messages again.

What is sent to draw and film

Covers and animated episodes are made from the fictional chapter and the fictional description of its characters. Your messages and your pictures are not sent to the image or video models.

Who processes it

Our server passes the material to the services that do the work and returns the result to your phone. We do not keep the messages, pictures or stories on our server and we do not write them to logs.

  • Writing: Anthropic's Claude models, reached directly or through fal.ai.
  • Covers and films: image and video models hosted by fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc.). A finished film is downloaded to your phone from a link the video service provides.
  • Hosting: Vercel Inc. runs our server. Like any web host it processes technical request data such as IP address and time in order to deliver the service.

These providers act on our instructions, may process data in the United States, and handle it under their own terms, which can include short-term retention for safety and abuse prevention. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Other people in your chat

A chat has two sides. Only bring a chat you are part of, with the other person's consent, and never one involving a minor. The stories are invented; they are not a record of what anyone said or did.

Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. We receive only what is needed to confirm a purchase.

Photo library

The app uses the system photo picker, so it only ever sees the pictures you pick.

Children

Ministories is not directed to children and is rated 13+. Do not use it with conversations or pictures of minors.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, the UK or another place with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we keep no accounts and store no content, we usually hold nothing to look up, but write to us and we will answer. We process what you send in order to provide the service you asked for.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

A contact address will be added here before the app is released.
