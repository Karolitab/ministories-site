Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Novelita writes fiction about two people from what you choose to bring: a chat, a couple of pictures, or a few lines. This page says plainly what happens to that material. Novelita is provided by TIM GABE HOLDING SOCIEDAD LIMITADA, Spain ("we"), which is the controller of the personal data described here.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • What you bring is sent to our server to be read once, and is not stored there.
  • A selection of it is sent to third-party AI services to write your story. The app asks for your permission before this happens.
  • Your stories, covers and films are kept in the app on your phone, not by us.
  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing is sent until you start a story and have given permission in the app.

  • A chat. The export file you pick is sent to our server over an encrypted connection. The server reads it in memory to find the two people, to choose a selection of days from across the whole conversation, and to count habits over every message (for example who writes first, at what hours, how fast you reply). The file is then discarded. It is not written to a disk, a database or a log.
  • The selection. That selection of messages, the two first names, the habit counts and any corrections you add are sent once to the AI writer, which returns a short description of the two of you. Your messages are never shown back, quoted in a story, or sent again. Later episodes are written from that description and the episodes so far.
  • Pictures. The one or two pictures you pick are sent once to an AI vision model that writes a line about each person (hair, skin tone, eyes) and, if you start from pictures, the description of the two of you. Only that text is kept, on your phone. The pictures themselves are not stored and are not sent to the image or video generators.
  • A few lines. The text you type, sent once to the AI writer.
  • Covers and films. Made from the fictional chapter and the text description of its characters. Your messages and pictures are not part of this.

Third-party AI and other processors

We use these services to do the work. Each receives only what is listed and handles it under its own terms:

  • Anthropic, PBC (Claude models): the selection of messages, names, habit counts, notes, pictures for description, and the story so far, to write. Privacy policy.
  • fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc.): the fictional chapter and character descriptions, to draw covers and render films; and, when it is used to reach the writing model, the same material as the writer above. Privacy policy.
  • Vercel Inc.: hosts our server, so everything the app sends passes through it in transit. It also processes technical request data such as IP address, time and path to deliver and protect the service. Privacy policy.
  • Apple: distributes the app and processes purchases.

We use these providers' business (API) services. They act as our processors, and may keep inputs for a limited time for safety and abuse prevention as their terms describe. We do not use your material to train AI models.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your library: stories, episodes, covers and downloaded films.
  • The description of the two of you, the character lines and your corrections.
  • Your settings, language and credit balance.

Deleting a story removes it. Deleting the app removes everything. We have no copy.

How long data is kept

  • By us: only for the seconds or minutes a request takes, in memory.
  • By our host: technical request logs (not content) for a limited period.
  • By the AI providers: as set out in their terms linked above.
  • Film files: a finished film sits at a link provided by the video service until your phone has downloaded it, and expires under that service's terms.

Why we are allowed to process it

Under the GDPR and similar laws we rely on: your consent, asked in the app before anything is sent to third-party AI, which you can withdraw at any time by not starting new stories or by deleting the app; performance of a contract, to deliver the story, cover or film you asked for; and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

Other people in your chat

A chat has two sides, so it contains another person's personal data. Only bring a chat you are part of, with the other person's consent, and never one involving a minor. The stories are invented; they are not a record of what anyone said or did. If you believe your messages were used without your consent, contact us. We store nothing, but we will help where we can.

Sensitive information

Chats can contain private details such as health, beliefs or intimate matters. We do not ask for this and the writer is instructed to keep stories free of it, but the selection is read by the AI writer. Only bring a chat you are comfortable having processed as described here.

Where data is processed

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. We receive only what is needed to confirm a purchase.

Permissions

The app uses the system photo and file pickers, so it only ever sees the pictures and the file you pick. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone or messages.

Tracking and sale of data

We do not track you across apps or websites, do not show ads, and do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in US state privacy laws such as the CCPA.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, handled in memory and not stored by us. No method is perfectly secure, so please bring only what you are comfortable sharing.

Children

Novelita is rated 13+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process children's personal data. Do not use it with conversations or pictures of minors.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es). Because we keep no accounts and store no content, we usually hold nothing to look up, but write to us and we will answer within one month.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the change affects what is sent to third parties, the app will ask for your permission again.

Contact

Questions, requests about your data, or a concern about a chat that includes you: novelita.app@gmail.com
