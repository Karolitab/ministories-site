Novelita support

Any idea, as a video. Your chat, as a series.

Novelita turns an idea into a short AI video, or your WhatsApp chat into a series you can read and watch, one episode at a time. If something is not working, or you have a question, write to novelita.app@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I make a video?

Type your idea in the box on Home, pick a look and tap the send button. Tap the plus button to add characters by name, and in the looks that draw real people, a picture of each.

How do I bring a chat?

In WhatsApp open the chat, tap the name at the top, choose Export Chat, then Without Media, and save it to Files. In Novelita tap the plus button in the box on Home, choose Use my chat and pick the file. A chat becomes a series.

What happens to what I bring?

After you give permission in the app, it is sent to our server, which passes it to our AI partners and stores nothing. From a chat, the export is read once in memory and only a selection of messages goes to the AI writer. Pictures stay on your phone and are sent to fal.ai with each film to draw the people in them. The Privacy Policy has the detail.

What are credits?

Reading is free. Making something new uses credits, and the price is shown before you confirm. Credits come in packs, paid once, with no subscription. If a story or film fails, the credits come back.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A film is taking a long time

An animated episode takes a few minutes to render. If it fails, the credits are returned and you can try again.

The story got something wrong

It is fiction, so it invents. Before writing, use the corrections box under "What we read" to fix names or facts, or rewrite an episode you would change.
