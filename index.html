Novelita support

Your story, as a series.

Novelita turns the two of you into a short novel you can read and watch, one episode at a time. If something is not working, or you have a question, write to novelita.app@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I bring a chat?

In WhatsApp open the chat, tap the name at the top, choose Export Chat, then Without Media, and save it to Files. In Novelita tap the plus button, choose WhatsApp chat and pick the file. No chat? Use a couple of pictures or a few lines instead.

What happens to my chat?

After you give permission in the app, the export is sent to our server, read once in memory and discarded. A selection of messages goes to our AI writing partners to describe the two of you, and the stories are written from that description. Nothing is stored on our side and your messages are never quoted. The Privacy Policy has the detail.

What are credits?

Reading is free. Making something new uses credits, and the price is shown before you confirm. Novelita Pro includes credits each period, and packs are sold on their own. If a story or film fails, the credits come back.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A film is taking a long time

An animated episode takes a few minutes to render. If it fails, the credits are returned and you can try again.

The story got something wrong

It is fiction, so it invents. Before writing, use the corrections box under "What we read" to fix names or facts, or rewrite an episode you would change.
